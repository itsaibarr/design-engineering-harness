## Cards (×4)

4. **interactive-cards** — @kaolti — *Why it works:* hover drives the data itself (animated bars,
   pies, glowing cylinders), so exploration is rewarded with meaning, not decoration.
   `cards` · `interactive` · `3d` · `tech`
5. **beveled-cards** — @RachitThakur146 — *Why it works:* three beveled cards on a dark grid whose

   textures makes the component lab itself the product demo. `cards` · `interactive` · `3d` · `web-design/3d`

## Portfolios & identity (×7)

Five site/identity pieces plus two motion-forward demos the gallery files with them.
